# Refund window
Customers may return goods within 14 days of purchase with the original receipt. Card refunds go back to the
same mada or credit card; cash refunds are only for cash sales.

# Partial refunds
A partial refund is issued as a ZATCA credit note that references the original invoice. The credit note gets its
own invoice UUID and its own ICV in the EGS chain; the original invoice is never edited.

# سياسة الاسترجاع
يحق للعميل إرجاع المنتجات خلال 14 يوماً من تاريخ الشراء مع الفاتورة الأصلية، ويتم الاسترجاع بنفس وسيلة الدفع.
